# Research notes

## STAX — what it is

**The Stanford Initiative for Business, Taxation, and Society** — a cross-disciplinary tax research group housed in the **Stanford Graduate School of Business** Research Hub. It brings together **Economics, Finance, and Accounting** to study how taxation affects companies, countries, and society, and to train the next generation of tax scholars (PhD students, postdocs, pre-docs, visiting scholars).

**Current site structure** (stax.stanford.edu): Home · News · Research · Events · Members. Uses Stanford GSB branding, cardinal red, a group-photo hero.

**Research categories:** Federal Tax Policy · State & Local Tax Policy · International Tax Policy · Energy & Environmental Policy. Research often uses confidential administrative tax data.

**People:** 3 faculty (Rebecca Lester, Joshua Rauh, Juan Carlos Suárez Serrato), ~15 team members (PhD students, postdocs, research fellows, a senior research manager), plus alumni.

**Events:** the "Tax Research Workshop" series (e.g. #13 May 15 2025, #12 Mar 13 2025).

## Reference 1 — GCAP (globalcapitalallocation.com)

- Blue banner header, **condensed uppercase display type**, letterspaced nav. Very monochrome + one accent.
- Tons of whitespace; minimal.
- **Research page (the part the client likes):** category tabs — *Full Research Papers / Short and White Papers / Non-Technical Briefs*. Each entry = uppercase letterspaced title with an underline, an **italic grey byline** (authors · Working Paper · date), then the abstract in **restrained, muted body text**. Calm and legible.
- **Takeaway:** small abstract type + category tabs + generous whitespace = the research listing STAX wants.

## Reference 2 — Stanford RegLab (reglab.stanford.edu)

- **Full-bleed Stanford building image** hero with a friendly rounded-sans headline in white; two-tone "RegLab" wordmark.
- Nav: About · News · Projects · Publications · Data · Work With Us · Contact + search.
- **About page (the part the client likes):** intro paragraph, then a **headshot grid grouped by role** — *team / summer institute / fellows & affiliates / faculty affiliates / supporters*. Each person = photo + name + title. Clean and scannable.
- A **"Work With Us"** page = the careers model.
- **Takeaway:** approachable, image-forward, clean people grid grouped by role.

## What the client dislikes about the current STAX site

- Abstract fonts **too big**; overall **too busy**. → Fix with restrained type sizes and whitespace.
- Wants it **minimal** and distinctly **not** a generic template.

## Design principles adopted

1. **One accent** (Stanford Cardinal `#8C1515`), otherwise near-monochrome.
2. **Small, calm abstracts** on Research; category filter tabs (GCAP).
3. **Role-grouped people grid** on About (RegLab).
4. **Careers ready to populate**, linking to Stanford's careers portal.
5. **Distinctive typography** per direction — no generic system fonts, no decorative pills, no purple gradients.
6. **Subtle scroll reveals**, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.

## Recommendation (starting point)

For a Stanford GSB research group, **Direction 01 "The Ledger"** (editorial) reads as the most scholarly and timeless, and most directly answers "small abstracts, not busy." **Direction 02 "The Grid"** is the safest bet if STAX wants to look modern and institutional alongside GCAP. **Direction 03 "Quarter"** is closest to RegLab's warmth. Final call is the client's — that's why all three are built.
