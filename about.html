The Stanford Tax Lab

About

The Stanford Tax Lab (STAX) contributes to better, more informed discussions about tax policy through balanced and rigorous research and teaching. Our cross-disciplinary team engages with researchers and practitioners from accounting, economics, finance, and law to produce cutting-edge research and train the next generation of scholars and business leaders.
