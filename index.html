Energy & Environmental · American Economic Review · 2025

Regulating Conglomerates: Evidence from an Energy Conservation Program in China

Qiaoyi Chen, Zhao Chen, Zhikuo Liu, Juan Carlos Suárez Serrato, Daniel Yi Xu

Studying a major Chinese energy-conservation program, the paper shows regulated firms cut output and shifted production to unregulated affiliates within their conglomerate rather than becoming more efficient, revealing how business-group structure blunts regulation.

State & Local · Journal of Accounting Research · 2025

Tax Subsidy Disclosure and Local Economic Effects

Lisa De Simone, Rebecca Lester, Aneesh Raghunandan

State and local business subsidies raise local employment when subject to internal government disclosure, while public disclosure has little effect, as agencies substitute toward other incentives or post stale information.

Federal · Working Paper · 2024

The Spiderweb of Partnership Tax Structures

Ryan Hess, Emily Black, Zaynah Javed, Jonathan Hennessy, Rebecca Lester, Jacob Goldin, Daniel E. Ho, Annette Portz

Tracing income through millions of partnership entities in U.S. tax data, the paper maps their structures from simple to elaborate multi-tier webs and shows that audits of complex partnerships yield roughly $20 assessed per $1 spent.
