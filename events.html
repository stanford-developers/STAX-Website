The Stanford Tax Lab

Events

STAX regularly organizes events to promote new academic research, provide opportunities for the next generation of tax scholars, and facilitate the exchange of knowledge among policymakers and practitioners. You can find information on recent events below.

We hold biannual conferences at the Stanford GSB to bring together tax scholars from economics, accounting, and other fields.
