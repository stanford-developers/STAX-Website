Rebecca Lester, Anthony Welsch

Using 3,500 business incentives worth $6.2 billion granted in Alabama from 2012 to 2024, the paper finds incentives raised recipient employment by 61 jobs on average, up to 247 for the largest, at roughly $77,000 per job.
